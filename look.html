OBSERVE / LESSON 01

Look.

Look at good work. Often. Closely.

Before the pencil moves,
let your eyes wander.

Drawing begins with noticing. Study where the light falls, how shapes overlap, and which details make an object feel like itself.

ONE-MINUTE EXERCISE

Choose an object nearby. Look at it for a full minute without drawing. Then sketch its biggest shapes first.
