Hope.

Ruin it. Try again.

One rough drawing
does not define you.

Bad drawings are part of the deal. You can learn from an awkward line, a strange shape, or a page that did not turn out how you imagined.

WHEN YOU FEEL STUCK

Pause. Find one thing that works. Turn the page and try again. You do not have to fix every drawing to keep improving.
