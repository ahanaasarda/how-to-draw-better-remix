PREPARE / LESSON 02

Pencil.

Sharpen your pencil. Prepare everything.

A small setup
is enough to begin.

You do not need fancy materials. Keep a few useful tools close so that starting feels easy instead of complicated.

YOUR STARTING KIT
  • A pencil you like holding
  • Paper or a sketchbook
  • An eraser, if you want one
  • A comfortable place with enough light
